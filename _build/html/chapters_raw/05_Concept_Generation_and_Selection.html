Where are major components and subsystems? oAircraft / spacecraft configuration affects all aspects of performance, size and weight • Want a large number of concepts oDo not want to miss very promising concepts oCannot address a large number of concepts in detail oWith little to no analysis, want to select most promising concepts for further development and comparison \n\n\n\n\n\nPurpose 3 • Will discuss two approaches in some detail oBrainstorming oPugh’s method • Other popular methods oMorphological chart for concept generation oWeighted objectives for concept selection#

mobility (RAM) vehicles for 2030 EIS look like for a firefighting scenario?” MakeSure your questions fit your MISSION STATEMENT , CONOPS and REQUIREMENTS \n\n\n\n\n\nBrainstorming 6 • Allow each member to write down / sketch ideas oConsider doing this individually before the group meeting oUse note cards or paper; one idea per card • Each member presents one idea at a time oNo criticism, “wild” ideas welcomed oTeam members try to build on ideas of others, or combine features of several ideas • Session should be “fun” oRelaxed atmosphere oAbout 30 minutes maximum time Make sketches , only few words , as needed · \n\n\n\n\n\nBrainstorming rules 7

  1. No criticism during the session

  2. A large quantity of ideas is wanted

  3. Seemingly crazy ideas are welcome

  4. Present ideas briefly and concisely

  5. Try to combine and improve on the ideas of others \n\n\n\n\n\nOther concept generation methods 8
