Risk definitions must be communicated across the project, and to the sponsor! • Likelihood scale for deep space robotic mission: o1 Very low (< 1%) o2 Low (between 1%-10%) o3 Moderate (between 10%-50%) o4 High (between 50%-80%) o5 Very likely (>80%) \n\n\n\n\n\nRisk ratings 20 • There is no standard definition given by NESC for the consequence scale; the following is a common interpretation for robotic planetary missions • Consequence scale (1 – 5) o 1 Minimal impact § Example: minor delay in obtaining non-critical data o 2 Minor impact § Loss of non-critical data, delay in critical events o 3 Moderate impact § Loss of science objective, failure to meet full mission success criteria o 4 Major impact § Loss of instrument, loss of critical mission data set, failure to meet minimum mission success criteria o 5 Mission catastrophic § Loss of mission, mission cancellation, failure to achieve any mission objectives I \n\n\n\n\n\nNASA 5x5 risk matrix 21 • The NASA standard 5x5 risk matrix is a tool that enables quick assessment of mission and development risks • Note that 5x1 is yellow, and 1x5 is green • Typically, attention is focused on yellow and red risks 5
