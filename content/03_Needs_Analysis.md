# Needs Analysis

## Stakeholder Elicitation
Before any engineering can begin, the true needs of the stakeholders must be understood. Stakeholders include the end-users, regulatory bodies (like the FAA), and maintenance crews. Needs are typically expressed in qualitative language and describe the operational problem to be solved (the Concept of Operations, or ConOps).

## Defining the Mission
A successful needs analysis translates ambiguous desires into a concrete mission profile. For a cargo UAV, this involves defining the payload capacity, range, turnaround time, and operational environment.

## System Integration
For the Regional Cargo UAV, stakeholder needs encompass not only the payload (50kg) and range (200km), but also the specific operational realities of a 3D-printed airframe. Needs analysis must capture the requirement for modular printability and material anisotropy. These needs will be formalized in the MATLAB Requirements Toolbox as the authoritative source of truth.
