# Risk Management

## Identifying and Quantifying Risk
Risk is defined as the product of the likelihood of an event and the severity of its consequence. Engineers use Risk Matrices to plot these values and prioritize mitigation efforts.

## Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)
FMEA is a bottom-up approach that examines every component, determines how it might fail, and analyzes the system-level effects. For each failure mode, a mitigation strategy must be developed.

## System Integration
Risk management for a 3D-printed UAV involves unique failure modes not seen in traditional aerospace design. Risks such as layer delamination, warping during the print process, or material degradation under UV exposure must be identified early. These risks will be formally tracked and mitigated through your System Composer architecture and FMEA tables.
