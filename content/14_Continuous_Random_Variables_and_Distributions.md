# Continuous Random Variables

## Probability Density Functions
Continuous random variables can take on any value within a range (e.g., the exact lifespan of a battery in hours). The Probability Density Function (PDF), $f(t)$, describes this behavior. The probability of falling within a specific range is the integral of the PDF.

## The Normal and Exponential Distributions
The Normal distribution models measurement errors and manufacturing tolerances, while the Exponential distribution frequently models the lifespan of electronic components with a constant failure rate.

## System Integration
Continuous variables like flight time, battery degradation, and cumulative stress on the 3D-printed wings are modeled using continuous distributions. These models will feed directly into your sizing scripts, ensuring the UAV meets the 100-flight lifecycle requirement.
