# Requirements Analysis (Part 1)

## Formulating "Shall" Statements
Requirements bridge the gap between qualitative stakeholder needs and quantitative engineering specifications. A well-formed requirement uses the word "shall" and must be unambiguous, verifiable, and achievable. 

## Traceability
Every requirement must trace back to a specific stakeholder need. This bidirectional traceability ensures no "orphan" requirements exist (features nobody asked for) and no stakeholder needs are ignored.

## System Integration
Translating the RCUAV stakeholder needs into formal 'Shall' statements is the foundation of our Digital Thread. In the Requirements Toolbox, you must specify structural requirements that account for the 'Print-to-Weight' trade-offs of materials like PLA+ or PETG, ensuring every requirement is verifiable and traceable to the mission ConOps.
