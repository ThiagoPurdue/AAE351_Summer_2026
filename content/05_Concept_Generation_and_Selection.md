# Concept Generation and Selection

## Exploring the Design Space
Once requirements are set, the team must explore the design space. Techniques like morphological matrices allow engineers to systematically combine different subsystem solutions (e.g., combining a flying wing configuration with hybrid propulsion) to generate novel concepts.

## Initial Down-Selection
Not all generated concepts are viable. Initial feasibility screening quickly eliminates concepts that violate the laws of physics or hard constraints (such as maximum takeoff weight limits).

## System Integration
When generating concepts for the RCUAV, the choice between Electric and Hybrid propulsion directly impacts the vehicle's mass fraction. Because 3D-printed structures typically have lower strength-to-weight ratios than composites, your concept selection must aggressively trade off propulsion weight against structural infill density using formal decision matrices.
