“The function of systems engineering is to guide the engineering of complex systems.”
-Systems Engineering Principles & Practice, Kossiakoff, Sweet, Seymour, Biemer
Two Views
①System engineer as process follower: define & track requirements, book-keep 
mass & power budgets, track status of problem reports & action items
②System engineer as a leader:  establish a vision for the mission development 
& implementation, maintain a big-picture view of the trade space, develop a 
deep understanding of project risk areas, serve as focal point for 
communication across project
“Systems engineering is the art and science of developing an operable system 
capable of meeting mission requirements within imposed constraints including (but 
not restricted to) mass, cost and schedule.”
- Michael D. Griffin, James R. French, Space Vehicle Design, 1991
